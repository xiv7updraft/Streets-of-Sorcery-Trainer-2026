## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **LA: Streets of Sorcery**. This trainer provides optional assistance for investigations, branching dialogue, relationship management, mind-reading sequences, and wizard duels. It is designed to help players explore different story routes and endings without replacing the game's core narrative choices.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore every investigation route, romance outcome, mind-reading sequence, magical duel, and ending with less repetition.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

LA: Streets of Sorcery is primarily a visual novel and investigation experience rather than a free-roaming action game. Use the trainer after entering an interactive scene, then return to the game to navigate dialogue, locations, and story prompts normally.

### Tools & Inventory

The game focuses on narrative investigation and clue progression rather than a traditional equipment system. Keep the trainer enabled only when changing supported case or progression values, and disable it before saving whenever possible.

### World & Environment

Investigations unfold through magical crime cases, interrogations, branching conversations, social activities, dating scenes, and decisions that influence relationships and endings. Use route and relationship options to experience alternate story developments.

### Combat & Weapons

Wizard duels are part of the game's magical detective structure. Activate duel assistance before a confrontation begins, then disable it after the encounter has ended to reduce the chance of conflicts with scripted events.
